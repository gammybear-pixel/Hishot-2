ABOUT HISHOT

HISHOT is a premier game design community founded in 2020. We're dedicated to nurturing the next generation of game developers through hands-on workshops, industry connections, and collaborative projects.

With a focus on innovation and creativity, we provide resources, mentorship, and opportunities for designers at all skill levels to grow their talents and expand their knowledge.

Our community brings together passionate individuals from diverse backgrounds, creating a vibrant ecosystem where ideas flourish and groundbreaking games are born.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What is HISHOT?

HISHOT is a community hub for game designers, developers, artists, and enthusiasts to learn, collaborate, and grow together in the field of game design.

Who can join HISHOT?

Anyone with an interest in game design or development can join our community - from beginners to professional game developers.

Is it possible to join HISHOT online?

Yes! We offer virtual memberships with access to online workshops, resources, and our community forums.

What are the benefits of joining HISHOT?

Members get access to workshops, networking events, industry connections, collaborative opportunities, mentorship, and our state-of-the-art facilities.
